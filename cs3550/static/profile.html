About Me

I cook the kind of food that feels like home, and wander the world from my stove. My recipes are weeknight-friendly, written with clear steps, sensible substitutions, and the occasional gram weight when it helps. I love cast iron, pantry staples, and dishes that scale for friends and neighbors. If you try something, tell me what you changed--variation is the point.
